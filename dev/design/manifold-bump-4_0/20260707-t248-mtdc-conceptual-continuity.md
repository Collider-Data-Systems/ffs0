## 1. The principle

Frank Zappa called it **conceptual continuity**: everything he ever made was one continuous work —
no riff discarded, every old joke and poodle resurfacing decades later in a new jacket, everything
referencing everything. He ran his house studio, the Utility Muffin Research Kitchen, on the same
rule: nothing is thrown away, because everything fits somewhere later.

mo:os holds the same principle as executable doctrine: **the log is append-only; nothing is ever
erased; every page counts in every fold that follows.** And 4.0 gave it a type: a **manifold**
(D1, ontology v4.0.0) is "the colimit of branch-episodes sharing one purpose-slug" — precisely,
at the two-level reading ratified in T218-E4: a workspace is the colimit of its branch-episodes,
a manifold the colimit of per-repo branches sharing a purpose. A manifold IS conceptual
continuity, formalized. The ontology's own `urn_example` for the type is
`urn:moos:manifold:my-tiny-data-collider`. This dossier is that example's episode record.
