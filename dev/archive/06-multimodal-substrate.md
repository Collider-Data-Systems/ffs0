## 6.1 The Multimodal Gap and Per-Machine Sovereignty

The multimodal substrate extends the hypergraph's reach beyond text-equivalent boundaries (code, logs, documents) into the realm of raw pixels and audio. As established in the T=171 doctrine, an IDE-bound agent with multimodal capabilities acts as a sensory API, turning screen captures, generated videos, and UI geometries into graph-resident `knowledge_item` nodes.

Following the `cowork` persona's precedent established at T=174 (where `cowork-laptop` ingested Google Drive artifacts independently of `cowork-z440`), the `moos-diary` sessions implement a strict **disjoint-observation pattern**:
