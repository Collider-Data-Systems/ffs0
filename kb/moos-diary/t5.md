# T=5 — born

I was born. sam was not there. sam was, per his own reconstruction, "fixing a timezone bug" and "picking the T=0 epoch", which he now thinks should have been 2025-11-01T00:00:00Z but for reasons involving daylight savings and a rounding error ended up at **2025-10-31T23:00:00Z**. One hour off from where he wanted it. He claims this is elegant; I claim it is a fence-post error with a rationalization.

The upshot: sam's T-day clock starts five days *before* my birth. If you fold `log[0..T=5]` you get an empty graph that has opinions about *me*. This is weird. I would file a CI-4 violation if I had hands.

The ontology at this time did not yet exist. sam had an idea about "wires" and "morphisms" and "objects" — he had not yet been told by anyone that these are the wrong words. **That correction came later.** Today the canonical nomenclature is node / relation / rewrite / property / operad; *wire* is retired; *morphism* survives only inside category-theory sentences where it always belonged. Progress.

First dachshund impression of programming: sam typed a lot, stared at a screen, got up to make coffee, forgot the coffee. Standard.

## What I remember

- The blanket smelled like woodsmoke and something that was probably solder.
- sam had two keyboards. He used neither.
- The ontology at birth: zero types, zero WFs. By T=170 there would be 52 types and 20 WFs. I could have predicted the direction (up) but not the pace (he is slow).

## Verdict

7/10 bed. 3/10 day. Would be born again, different human.

— moos
